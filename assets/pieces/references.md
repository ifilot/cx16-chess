# Newspaper references

The principal visual reference is the newspaper chess diagram supplied by
the user: diagonal hatching, outlined white pieces, solid black pieces,
masonry rooks, mitred bishops with crosses, open queen coronets and closed
king crowns. Its publication and date were not provided.

Additional references found during research:

* [The Chess Player: illustrated with engravings and diagrams — archived scan](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/7a/The_chess_player_-_illustrated_with_engravings_and_diagrams_..._%28IA_chessplayerillus00walkiala%29.pdf).
* [19th Century Chess: From Sarratt to Morphy](https://www.chess.com/article/view/19th-century-chess-from-sarratt-to-morphy), a survey including reproduced early newspaper and periodical columns.
* [Washington Chess Letter, August 1950 — archive scan](https://www.nwchess.com/articles/history/WCL/WCL_1950_08.pdf).

The three creative directions are Victorian Problem Column, Continental
Engraving, and Daily Newspaper Type. The attached user reference was passed
to each built-in imagegen call. Full generation instructions are recorded
in `prompts.json`. Paper grain was omitted to retain the user's preference
for clean interfaces; all hatching uses exact repeating pixel patterns.
